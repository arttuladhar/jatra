INSPIRED BY THE NEWARI CHARIOT TRADITIONBUILD WITH CARE. PULL TOGETHER.

Carry it together.

Touch: Swipe the scene to orbit; pinch to zoom. In the workshop, tap ↑ / ↓ to select parts and ← / → to customize, then tap Begin the procession. Hold Pull to build effort and hold Left or Right with another finger to steer. Release Pull to coast; hold Brake to slow down. Tap Pause to stop or resume, and Workshop to rebuild. Panels scroll to reveal more details.

Workshop: ↑ / ↓ select a part; ← / → change construction. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Enter begins.

Procession: Drag the scene to orbit the camera and scroll to zoom. Use Overhead view to see over buildings; Reset view restores the default angle. The camera stays centered on the chariot, including while paused. Hold W to build pulling effort. ← / → turn the chariot left / right, even from rest. You can also hold the on-screen Pull, Left / Right, and Brake buttons. The steering readout shows the direction received. If both are held, the last direction pressed wins. Hold Space to progressively apply Ma eel brakes. Release to coast. P pauses. R returns to the workshop.

Sound: The procession begins with sound at a moderate volume. Use Mute sound and the Volume slider in the header. Audio pauses with P, the controls dialog, or a hidden tab, and stops when returning to the workshop.

Follow the gold checkpoints in order through 8 m streets and tight bends. Watch the next-turn distance, brake early, and keep the whole chariot on the road. Leaving the road progressively damages joints; returning stops further damage but does not repair it. A broken joint or a tipped chariot ends the attempt; the physics keeps running so you can see the collapse.
